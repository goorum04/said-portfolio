FAQ

Can you tell it is made with AI?

The visuals are generated and I label them (Instagram requires it). What matters is that the message is yours and the video stops the scroll. If you have real photos or videos of your premises, I combine them and it's even better.

Do I have to appear in the video?

No. No reel requires a camera or actors. If you want to appear, that's possible too, but not necessary.

What if I don't like a reel?

Each reel includes one revision. If it still doesn't convince you after the revision, it doesn't count and I make another one.
