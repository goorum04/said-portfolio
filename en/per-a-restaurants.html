FAQ

Does the reel look AI-made?

The images of your place are your real photos; AI adds motion, voice and editing. The result looks like an ad, not a cartoon.

Can the website really be in four languages?

Yes, with one page per language that Google indexes separately (not a machine-translation button). You edit the menu from a panel and it gets translated.

What do I need to start with reels?

An Instagram account and ten minutes on WhatsApp to tell me what to highlight this month. Photos of the place if you have them; if not, I make them with AI from what exists.
