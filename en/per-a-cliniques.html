The problem

Three things that cost money every week

No-shows

10–20% of appointments neither kept nor cancelled. Every gap in the diary is a paid hour with no income. An automatic reminder the day before halves them.

Booking by phone

Reception taking calls to give appointments while the client in front of them waits. And at 9 pm, when people book, nobody answers.

Health data in a spreadsheet

Histories, injuries, allergies: special-category data under the LQPD. In a shared sheet with no access log, it's the first thing the APDA would look at.

FAQ

Do we need to publish on the App Store?

No. It installs from a link and keeps an icon on the phone (PWA). You skip Apple and Google fees and reviews. If you ever want it in the stores, it can be packaged.

Are WhatsApp reminders legal?

Yes, if the client gave their number for that purpose and can opt out. Done through the official WhatsApp Business API with approved templates, not a phone sending bulk messages.

What if we already have clinic software?

Usually I connect the reminders and client app without touching it: it stays the main system, the client gets an app and you get a diary without gaps.
