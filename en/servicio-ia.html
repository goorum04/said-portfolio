CRM d'expedients · en ús

At the firm where I work, every foreign-investment file goes through twenty steps, three administrations and a pile of expiring documents. For years that was handled with Excel and shared folders. I built the CRM we use now, and that's what I offer you: adapted to your firm, not a template.

AI agents are the second layer: an assistant that answers from your documents (RAG) and agents that sort email, prepare checklists and flag what expires. Connected to the CRM, with a log of everything they do, and no data leaving Europe.

What's included

  • Case-file CRM: clients, KYC, procedures with steps, deadline calendar, passport and policy alerts, document templates.
  • Client portal: clients see what is missing and upload it from their phone, via a secure link, no account to remember.
  • RAG assistant: answers questions from your documents and cites the source. If it doesn't know, it says so.
  • Agents with tasks: email, checklists, deadlines, drafts. Each with a role and an activity log.
  • Integrations: HubSpot, email, calendar, meeting transcription, e-signature.
  • Data protection by design: minimisation, access logs, rate limits, data in Europe, processing register.

Who it's for

Accounting, advisory and law firms, property managers, estate agencies and any SME in Andorra that manages files, documents and deadlines. If you run it today on Excel, folders and email, this is for you.

FAQ

Is the software mine or do I pay a licence?

It's yours: code, database and access. You pay for development and, optionally, monthly maintenance. If you ever leave, you take everything with you.

Where is the data stored?

On servers in Europe (or in Andorra if you prefer). The AI models are not trained on your data and every query is logged.

How long does it take?

A RAG assistant, two to three weeks. A complete CRM, six to ten weeks with weekly deliveries so you start using it early.

What if I already have HubSpot or another CRM?

We connect them. The case-file CRM does what HubSpot doesn't (procedures, KYC, deadlines) and HubSpot keeps doing what it does well.

Tell me how you handle your files today.

A WhatsApp message is enough. I'll tell you in one reply whether I can fix it, how long it would take and what it would cost. If I'm not the right person, I'll say so.
