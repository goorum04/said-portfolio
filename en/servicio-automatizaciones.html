FAQ

Where do the automations run?

On a server in Europe that I manage, or on yours if you prefer. Data does not go through third-party services we have not agreed on.

What if it fails?

I get an alert and so do you. The work is not lost: it stays queued and is retried or done by hand.

Tell me a weekly task that bores you.

A WhatsApp message is enough. I'll tell you in one reply whether I can fix it, how long it would take and what it would cost. If I'm not the right person, I'll say so.
