cas CASS · novembre 2026

In November 2026 an attacker emptied the CASS business portal with four stolen certificates and 1.2 million queries: 477,238 NIA with name and date of birth. Not a genius hacker: a screen that returned data from an identifier, with no rate limit and nobody reading the logs.

Any portal with a "search by ID" has the same problem. And in Andorra, Law 29/2021 requires adequate measures, notifying a breach to the APDA within 72 hours and informing those affected. I am a lawyer and a developer: I review both in one engagement.

What's included

  • Minimisation: no screen or API returns personal data from an identifier.
  • Rate limits and alerts: rate limiting, attempt blocking, automatic alerts.
  • Credentials and access: two-factor, session expiry, least privilege.
  • Access logs that exist, do not fill the disk, and someone reads.
  • Processing register and breach response plan (72 h, APDA) with notification templates.
  • 5–10 page report with traffic lights and a 45-minute meeting with the decision-maker.

Who it's for

Firms, estate agencies, clinics, insurers, property managers, schools, gyms with an app, and any company in Andorra with a client area, employee portal or app showing personal data. I work with a test user, read-only, never with real data.

FAQ

Will you access my system or touch real data?

No. I work with a test user you create for me, read-only. No aggressive intrusion tests, no downloading client data. If something deeper is needed, we agree it in writing first.

My site was built by another company. Does it still work?

Yes, that's the most common case. The report is written so your current provider can apply the fixes. No need to switch providers.

Is this an official audit or a certification?

No. It is a practical review to reduce real risk and have the documentation Law 29/2021 requires. It does not replace a certified audit (ISO 27001, formal pentest) if your sector requires one.

What if I have already had a data breach?

Write to me today. The 72-hour clock to notify the APDA is already running; the first thing is to document what happened and prepare the notification and the message to those affected.

Does your portal return client data?

A WhatsApp message is enough. I'll tell you in one reply whether I can fix it, how long it would take and what it would cost. If I'm not the right person, I'll say so.
