FAQ

Where is my firm's data?

In Andorra or the EU, with the provider we agree on (named in the contract). Never in the US without safeguards, and never used to train AI models.

Can I start with just deadlines?

Yes. It removes the most pain and is the fastest to set up. Files and the portal are added later without starting over.

What if we already have software?

It's usually Spanish or French software that doesn't know Andorran foreign investment or the LQPD, with a spreadsheet alongside. I can build just the missing part and connect it to what you use.

What if I stop working with you?

Code and data are yours from day one, documented. Any developer can continue it. No lock-in, no per-user licence.

Want to see the CRM with one of your own files?

A WhatsApp message is enough. I'll tell you in one reply whether I can fix it, how long it would take and what it would cost. If I'm not the right person, I'll say so.
